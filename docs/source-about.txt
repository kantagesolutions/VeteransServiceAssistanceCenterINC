With a dedication to those who served, Veteran Service & Assistance, Inc. is driven to connect veterans to the vital resources that will help them rebuild their lives.All About Our StoryFounder, Retired SSG Tonya L. Price, has served in the US Army for 20 years. During her service, she served those doing what she loved as a Behavioral Health Specialist. Since retirement, she has used her knowledge and experience from her time in active duty to help those become educated on what benefits they are eligible for as well as helping fellow soldiers, even after their service. She has strived to do as much as possible for those who have found themselves in difficult situations, such as losing their home or becoming unemployed. Retired SSG Price has dedicated countless time to help and has helped over 2,000 veterans within the CSRA as well in other states by guiding them into the right direction whether she could help or knew someone who could.
